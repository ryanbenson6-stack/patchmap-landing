Since you signed up

A lot has changed since you signed up.

Most of it happened on the phone. The part of PatchMap you were most likely to give up on is the part that changed most.

Start from the spreadsheet you already have. Pick the file on your phone, get a patch sheet.

Then actually work it from your pocket. Moving through a show no longer means a laptop.

  • The phone is a real surface now. Not a shrunken desktop you pinch around — screens built for a hand, in a dark room, in a hurry.
  • Spreadsheet to patch sheet in one pass. Point it at the file you already keep the show in. No retyping a channel list to find out whether this is for you.
  • Start on your phone, finish on your desktop. The same show, picked up where you left it — the handoff stopped being a re-import.
