We're Aggressively Anti–Data Entry.

PatchMap is node-based at its core. That frees you from entering data at all — the nodes already understand what they are. Patching a 60-seat orchestra? There's no way you have time to type that much.

PatchMap has templates, show imports, and Smart Import — we just don't make you type your way through them. The system is auto-labeling and self-resolving. Assign your patch once — every role, every device — and it still holds tomorrow. When something changes, that's fine.

Smart Import

Import the Roster. Patch Nothing Until You Say So.

Drop a CSV, an Excel sheet, or a PDF — Smart Import reads the gear inside it, understands the position and the channel you're placing it in, so you don't have to repatch anything. It also checks the gear you already have listed and asks whether you want to add it to your locker or your rental inventory — one-step import.

It builds a staging shelf with the exact nodes it found, correctly counted, named the way you already had them.

Most "smart importers" populate a list but require you to re-patch everything by hand. We're anti-data entry.

If your string section starts at channel 21, PatchMap waits for channel 21 to become available and pages itself there automatically. There's no need to patch things twice — that would make it a dumb importer.
